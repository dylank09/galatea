Use often

  • Charcoal, ink navy, dark denim, and mulberry near the face.
  • Soft cool white, pearl, and pale blue-grey as light neutrals.
  • Medium to medium-high contrast, such as soft white with navy.
  • Silver, white gold, cool steel, cool pearls, and medium-scale prints.

Keep away from the face

  • Warm cream, camel, tan, golden beige, rust, tomato red, mustard, peach, and warm olive.
  • Optic white and solid black as the default top or turtleneck.
  • Neon brights, muddy earth tones, and very warm small florals.

Fit and proportion

Fit the hips first. Keep the torso clear.

Tops and knitwear

  • Actual shoulder seam, soft crew, boat, shallow square, or open collar.
  • Heavyweight tees and fine-to-medium knits, ending at the waistband or decisively below the fullest hip.
  • Use cropped layers and neat overshirts to preserve a long leg line without crowding the short torso.

Trousers and denim

  • Default to mid-rise, near or just below the navel.
  • Choose straight or gently wide legs, a clean hip line, and curvy/contoured waistbands where possible.
  • Start around AU 6–8 for tops and AU 8 for bottoms. Try AU 10 if the hip is tight, then tailor the waist.

Skirts, dresses, and layers

  • Favour A-line, bias, or gently waist-defined shapes.
  • Use cropped denim jackets, short chore jackets, neat overshirts, or an unstructured navy blazer.
  • Choose deliberate ankle-skimming, knee, or midi hems, never an accidental widest-calf cutoff.

Immediate no

  • Ultra-high rises, waist gaps, tight hips/seat, extreme dropped shoulders, or very bulky knits.
  • Huge cargo pockets, very stiff full skirts, empire waists, and oversized blazers.
  • Anything that requires a belt to stay up, has the wrong shoulder fit, or is uncomfortable in the change room.
Tailor only the simple parts. Hems and a small waist adjustment are worth it. Do not buy hoping to fix the rise, shoulders, hip tension, scratchy fabric, or a colour that is wrong near the face.

Outfit formulas

Two modes, one shared wardrobe.

Feminine

  • Soft-white tee + dark straight jeans + cropped denim jacket + silver jewellery + grey sneaker.
  • Berry fine knit + charcoal trousers + almond-toe ballet flat.
  • Blue-and-soft-white stripe top + navy A-line skirt + minimal sandal.
  • Cool navy dress + cool-grey cardigan + black or silver ballet flat.

Androgynous

  • Soft-blue shirt, partial tuck + ink-navy wide trouser + low-profile sneaker.
  • Charcoal tee + navy overshirt + dark straight jeans + Sambas.
  • Navy knit polo + charcoal trouser + neat blazer + loafer or ballet flat.
  • Cool-white tee + slate trousers + short chore jacket + silver accessories.

Shoes and accessories

Keep footwear low-profile and versatile.

Four-shoe module

  • Soft-white, cool-grey, or navy low-profile sneaker.
  • Clean charcoal, navy, or cool-grey runner.
  • Minimal black or cool-grey sandal with clean straps.
  • Black, deep navy, or silver almond-toe ballet flat or loafer.

Bag and jewellery module

  • Charcoal, navy, or black small shoulder/crossbody bag.
  • Cool-grey, navy, or black structured tote.
  • Silver jewellery, cool pearls, and clean medium-scale geometric forms.
Avoid warm brown bags, tan sandals, very chunky trainers, and crossbody straps ending at the fullest hip.

Tailor

Release

Warm near-face colours, waist-gap pants, bulky sweaters, or one-outfit pieces.

Test strategically

Keep lower-rise jeans if they sit smoothly and work with untucked tees or short knits. Keep high-rise only if they do not crowd the short torso.
