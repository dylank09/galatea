Fit and proportion

Fit the hips first. Keep the torso clear.

Tops

Clear the torso

Shape
True shoulder seam · soft crew · boat · shallow square · open collar
Fabric
Heavy tee · fine-to-medium knit
Length
At the waistband or clearly below the fullest hip

Fit hips first

Ultra-high riseWaist gapTight hipsDropped shoulders Bulky knitsHuge cargosEmpire waistOversized blazer
