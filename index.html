Flexible weekly rule

Follow Lower A → Upper A → Lower B → Upper B. Do not cram missed sessions together. Aim for no more than two lifting days in a row and about 48 hours between the two lower sessions.

Shift-work priority: Place Lower A or Lower B after your best sleep. On an exhausting 12-hour shift or after a poor night, use the shortened session, reduce load 5–10%, or rest. Do not chase a missed workout.

Keep it at least 24 hours before a hard lower session if it is leg-heavy. Skip it during Week 8 deload, consecutive night shifts and high-fatigue weeks.

      Guidance

      Double progression

      1. Start at the lower end of the prescribed rep range at the assigned RIR.
      2. Add repetitions with the same load.
      3. When every set reaches the top of the range at target RIR, add the smallest practical load increment next session.
      4. If technique deteriorates, reps fall below the range, or readiness is poor, hold or reduce. Do not force progression.

      Most compound lifts use 2–3 minutes rest; unilateral and machine exercises use 90–120 seconds; isolation and core use 60–90 seconds.

      Endometriosis and lower-energy days

      Use symptoms, not a rigid cycle phase, to decide adjustments. On 1–2 lower-energy or painful days, remain at 3–4 RIR, reduce sets or load, choose stable machines and low-impact warm-up cardio, and avoid any bracing, range or impact that aggravates symptoms. Resume normally when symptoms settle.

      Seek clinical advice for new or escalating pelvic pain, fainting, unusually heavy bleeding, neurological symptoms or symptoms persisting beyond your usual pattern.

      Evidence and programme rationale

      Progressive resistance training, sufficient weekly quality volume, sets performed reasonably near failure, and progressive overload are the main evidence-based levers for hypertrophy and strength. Hip thrusts and squats can both grow glutes, so the plan uses multiple hip- and knee-dominant patterns rather than claiming one universally best exercise. No exercise spot-reduces abdominal fat.
