Care-plan tasks

What the item is asking you to do

Develop a plan · document · evaluate · help the parent determine goals · take a history · work with other healthcare providers · visually examine nipple and breast · visually examine infant position and latch · communicate with families.

Chronological periods

When the dyad is being seen

Prenatal · labour and birth · prematurity · 0–2 days · 3–14 days · 15–28 days · 1–3 months · 4–6 months · 7–12 months · beyond 12 months · general principles.

How to use this: After every mock, compare your score by discipline, task, and period. Study the lowest meaningful category, then retest it. Do not treat the blueprint as a prediction of any hidden live form.
