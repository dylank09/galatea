Clinical verification

References for explanations

Practice explanations point to Core Curriculum for Interdisciplinary Lactation Care, Breastfeeding and Human Lactation, Hale's Medications and Mothers' Milk, ABM protocols, WHO and UNICEF guidance, AAP guidance, CDC storage guidance, and LactMed. Verify the current edition, revision date, and local policy before applying time-sensitive advice.

Especially verify: mastitis-spectrum care, medication exposure, cannabis and alcohol, safe sleep, complementary feeding, and any condition requiring diagnosis or prescribing.

What is not here

No recent exam papers

IBLCE does not publish recent examination papers. This site contains original practice items only. Avoid leaked content and “exam dumps”; examination security protects the credential and public safety.

Score context

Benchmarks are historical

The app shows a historical 132/175 raw benchmark and older cohort means for study context. Current score reports use scaled scores, so practice percentages do not convert directly to a live result.
