Question anatomy

How to read the item

1. Find the period. A 5-day-old, a late-preterm infant, or a parent returning to work changes the decision.

2. Find the task. “First”, “best”, “evaluate”, “ask”, “refer”, and “document” are not decoration.

3. Protect scope and safety. The IBCLC supports, assesses, educates, documents, collaborates, and refers. Do not independently diagnose, prescribe, or order.

4. Compare timing. Many distractors are good actions at the wrong time. Choose the best next response for this dyad and goal.

Part 2: Most items are associated with an image, chart, or graph. In this offline bank, image items use a clearly labelled study diagram or description, never a licensed clinical photograph.

Exam mechanics

  • 175 questions, four options, one best response.
  • Four-hour appointment includes administration and one scheduled break.
  • Review is allowed within a part; Part 1 cannot be reopened after submission.
  • Practice target: about 1.2 minutes per item.
