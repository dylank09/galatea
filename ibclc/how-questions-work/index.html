Published format

What to expect

  • 175 multiple-choice questions with four response choices.
  • One best response, delivered by computer.
  • Four-hour appointment including administration and one scheduled break.
  • Part 1 is text-only. Part 2 is predominantly associated with images, charts, or graphs.
  • You can review within a part, but Part 1 is locked after submission.

The five-step read

Slow down in the right place

  1. Find the period. Age, prematurity, pregnancy, or return to work changes the decision.
  2. Find the task. Notice “first”, “best”, “evaluate”, “ask”, “refer”, or “document”.
  3. Name the goal. Is the item asking for assessment, education, a plan, referral, or evaluation?
  4. Protect scope. Remove options that diagnose, prescribe, or order outside the IBCLC role.
  5. Choose timing. Prefer the safest best-next action for this dyad now.

Answer choices

Why all four options can sound reasonable

Distractor patternHow to spot it
Right action, wrong timingIt may be useful later, but the lead-in asks for the first or next step.
True but not indicatedIt is a real fact or intervention that does not address this dyad's stated goal.
Outside scopeIt independently diagnoses, prescribes, or orders instead of assessing, educating, collaborating, or referring.
Old guidanceIt matches familiar advice but conflicts with a current protocol or reference.
Overly absoluteWords such as “always”, “never”, or “must” often ignore individual context.

Pacing

Use about 1.2 minutes per item

The practical content target is about 210 minutes for 175 items. Mark difficult items and return within the current part rather than spending several minutes proving one answer.

Current guidance

Verify before memorising

Older books and newer guidance can differ. Check current sources for mastitis-spectrum care, medication exposure, cannabis and alcohol, safe sleep, and complementary feeding.
