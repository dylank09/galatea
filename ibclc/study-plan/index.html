The sprint

One useful task each day

  1. 1Diagnostic. Take the 50-item set without notes. Record confidence and time.
  2. 2Development and Nutrition. Review growth, feeding behaviour, prematurity, anatomy, and complementary feeding.
  3. 3Pharmacology. Review alcohol, nicotine, cannabis, contraception, medications, galactagogues, scans, and referral.
  4. 4Physiology. Review milk production, diabetes, endocrine disorders, relactation, induction, and hypoglycaemia.
  5. 5Part 1 drill. Take an 87-item timed drill and analyse task wording and pacing.
  6. 6Pathology. Review mastitis spectrum, nipple pain, supply, jaundice, reflux, ankyloglossia, and referral thresholds.
  7. 7Clinical Skills. Review communication, consent, confidentiality, WHO Code, research, pumps, scales, and storage.
  8. 8Full mock. Sit all 175 items under exam conditions, including the break.
  9. 9Weak areas. Use the report to drive targeted drills and image/chart practice.
  10. 10Light review. Do a 25-item confidence set, revisit current-guidance cards, and protect sleep.

The review loop

Turn misses into marks

  • What did the stem actually ask?
  • Which clue determined the period?
  • Why is the keyed answer best now?
  • Why is each distractor tempting but wrong?
  • Which source will you verify?

Last-minute check

Practise the mechanics

  • Four choices, one best response.
  • Locked Part 1.
  • Charts and images before answer choices.
  • Referral mindset: assess, support, educate, document, collaborate.
