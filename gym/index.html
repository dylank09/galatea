Flexible weekly rule

Follow Lower A → Upper A → Lower B → Upper B. Do not cram missed sessions together. Aim for no more than two lifting days in a row and about 48 hours between the two lower sessions.

Shift-work priority: Place Lower A or Lower B after your best sleep. On an exhausting 12-hour shift or after a poor night, use the shortened session, reduce load 5–10%, or rest. Do not chase a missed workout.

Optional fifth day

It is optional conditioning, not a strength-session replacement. Skip it during Week 8 deload, consecutive night shifts and high-fatigue weeks.

      Plan tools

      Guidance

      Double progression

      1. Start at the lower end of the prescribed rep range with controlled technique.
      2. Add repetitions with the same load.
      3. When every set reaches the top of the range, add the smallest practical load increment next session.
      4. If technique deteriorates, reps fall below the range, or readiness is poor, hold or reduce. Do not force progression.

      Rest is set to the shortest window that still protects your reps: 2 minutes after the main glute and hinge lifts, 90 seconds after the other compounds, 75–90 seconds for machine and unilateral work, and 45–60 seconds for isolation and core.

      Rest is a floor, not a ceiling. If reps drop below the bottom of the prescribed range, take more rest before you consider reducing the load — use +30s on the timer. Breathless, rushed sets cost you more than an extra minute does.

      Keep the main lifts as straight sets. Only the accessory pairs boxed and badged ⇄ Superset in a session are meant to be alternated: do a set of the first, a set of the second, then take one rest before the next round. Use that pair's shared Rest after both timer rather than resting twice. If a machine is busy or you feel rushed, run them as straight sets instead.

      Glutes, thighs and waist

      Lower-body volume is deliberately biased toward hip extension and hip abduction. The two low-volume lunge sets maintain basic knee strength, but are not a thigh-growth priority. If the optional class has hard squats, lunges or jumps, skip it or treat it as the week’s knee-dominant work.

      Reducing thigh-training volume reduces the stimulus to add thigh muscle, but does not selectively remove thigh fat. A smaller waist or thigh measurement requires overall body-fat change where appropriate. A modest, sustainable energy deficit supports that goal better than aggressive restriction, which can compromise recovery and glute progress.

      Cycle context, endometriosis and lower-energy days

      Use cycle stage as context, not a prescription. With irregular cycles, do not rely on calendar dates to identify ovulation. Use symptoms, readiness and personal movement triggers to decide adjustments. On 1–2 lower-energy or painful days, reduce sets or load, choose stable machines and low-impact warm-up cardio, and avoid any bracing, range or impact that aggravates symptoms. Resume normally when symptoms settle.

      Pelvic-floor care is not always strengthening. Before the pelvic-health assessment, choose only comfortable relaxation and mobility work from the Pelvic care card below. Food supports training, but no food pattern treats endometriosis or confirms a cycle stage. A copper IUD can make bleeding heavier for some people. Seek clinical advice for persistent fatigue, breathlessness, dizziness, unusually heavy or prolonged bleeding, and before taking an iron supplement.

      Seek clinical advice for new or escalating pelvic pain, fainting, unusually heavy bleeding, neurological symptoms or symptoms persisting beyond your usual pattern.

      Evidence and programme rationale

      Progressive resistance training, sufficient weekly quality volume, sets performed reasonably near failure, and progressive overload are the main evidence-based levers for hypertrophy and strength. This plan uses more hip-extension and hip-abduction work to prioritise glutes while keeping knee-dominant volume low. That reduces the thigh-growth stimulus but neither lower-body nor core exercises spot-reduce fat.
